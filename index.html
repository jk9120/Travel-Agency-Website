Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions regarding holiday bookings, vouchers, and flights.

How do I book a tour package or customized trip?

You can easily browse our tour packages under Tour Package Deals or select any destination from Country Guides. Click "Book Package" or use the "Register Now" button at the top to submit your details. Our holiday specialist will confirm your itinerary immediately!

What payment methods do you support?

We accept all major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking across 50+ banks, and international wire transfers with 0% hidden convenience fees.

When will I receive my flight e-tickets and hotel vouchers?

Immediately after booking confirmation, digital e-tickets and verified hotel booking vouchers with QR codes are dispatched directly to your registered email address and WhatsApp number within 15 minutes.

Can I customize an itinerary or add extra days?

Yes! All our tour packages are 100% customizable. You can extend your stay, upgrade hotel categories (4-Star/5-Star/Resort), or add private guided excursions by letting our travel desk know via phone or email.
